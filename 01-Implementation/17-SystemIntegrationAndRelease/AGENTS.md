# AGENTS

This file applies to `01-Implementation/17-SystemIntegrationAndRelease`.

- Integration SHALL verify architecture rather than redefine it.
- Release SHALL preserve identity, authority, provenance and user ownership.
- Every persistent change SHALL have tested migration and recovery behavior.
- A release SHALL NOT proceed with unresolved critical security, privacy, integrity or data-loss risks.
- Upgrade and rollback SHALL preserve Master Library, Local Libraries and Personal Knowledge boundaries.
- Release artifacts SHALL be reproducible, signed and traceable.
- Quality gates SHALL use evidence rather than declaration alone.
