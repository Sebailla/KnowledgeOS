# AGENTS

This file applies to `01-Implementation/16-ExportAndPublishing`.

- UDM, DPM and Knowledge Objects SHALL remain canonical.
- Every export and publication output SHALL remain derived and regenerable.
- Export SHALL NOT mutate canonical or Personal Knowledge.
- Personal Knowledge inclusion SHALL be explicit and opt-in.
- Lossy transformations SHALL produce a loss report.
- Export profiles, manifests and public contracts SHALL be versioned.
- Plugin exporters SHALL use Plugin SDK public contracts and approved capabilities only.
