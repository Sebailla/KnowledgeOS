# AGENTS

This file applies to `01-Implementation/10-KnowledgeGraph`.

- Graph persistence SHALL remain derived and rebuildable.
- Canonical, Personal, Derived and External authority layers SHALL remain distinct.
- UDM and Knowledge Objects remain authoritative for source-backed semantics.
- Personal relationships remain user-owned.
- AI and similarity edges SHALL remain derived until explicitly accepted.
- Projection SHALL be deterministic and idempotent for fixed inputs and versions.
- Graph storage SHALL NOT redefine Domain identity.
