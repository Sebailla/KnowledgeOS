# AGENTS

This file applies to `01-Implementation/12-AIAndAssistance`.

- AI is optional.
- AI output SHALL remain derived or Personal Knowledge until explicitly accepted.
- Remote model use SHALL require explicit privacy policy authorization.
- Local models SHOULD be preferred when privacy or offline operation requires them.
- Provider, model, prompt, configuration and input provenance SHALL be recorded.
- AI SHALL NOT become canonical authority automatically.
- Personal Knowledge SHALL NOT be sent remotely without explicit authorization.
