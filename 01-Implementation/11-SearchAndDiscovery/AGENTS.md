# AGENTS

This file applies to `01-Implementation/11-SearchAndDiscovery`.

- Search indexes SHALL remain derived and rebuildable.
- Search results SHALL preserve Domain identity, authority, provenance and scope.
- Local Library, Master Catalog and Personal Knowledge search scopes SHALL remain distinct.
- Ranking SHALL NOT change canonical meaning or authority.
- Personal Knowledge SHALL be indexed and queried only under approved privacy scope.
- Remote providers SHALL not receive unauthorized Personal Knowledge.
- Search UI SHALL use public Search contracts and SHALL NOT access indexes directly.
