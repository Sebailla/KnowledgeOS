# AGENTS

This file applies to `01-Implementation/13-AnnotateAndAuthoring`.

- Publications, UDM and DPM SHALL remain immutable.
- Annotations, highlights, notes, drawings and reading marks belong to Personal Knowledge.
- Personal Knowledge SHALL NOT be written to the NAS Master Library.
- Every annotation SHALL use stable anchors and preserve anchor history.
- Conflicts SHALL preserve competing versions until merge.
- Ink and attachments SHALL use personal asset identities.
- Synchronization SHALL remain independent from publication acquisition.
