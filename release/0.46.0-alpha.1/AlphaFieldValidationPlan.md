# Alpha Field Validation Plan

The 0.46.0-alpha.1 cycle validates operational stability without adding product capabilities.

Evidence categories:

- repeated startup and shutdown;
- offline and reconnect behavior;
- synchronization convergence;
- import, reader, annotation, search, graph, AI and export smoke tests;
- NAS backup and isolated restore exercises;
- sanitized diagnostics;
- performance trend comparison;
- defect severity and release-gate review.

Physical-device, TestFlight and real NAS evidence must be collected externally and attached before promotion.
