# Known Issues — 0.45.0-alpha.1

- Apple signing, notarization and TestFlight delivery require project credentials and provisioning profiles.
- Share Extension, background execution, battery behavior and biometric lock require physical-device validation.
- PDF and EPUB export need broader format-conformance testing.
- Large-library performance baselines are preliminary.
- Remote AI is opt-in and must not receive restricted documents.
- Closed Alpha diagnostics are local and disabled by default.
