
# Architecture Decision Records

## Purpose

Architecture Decision Records (ADR) document the most important architectural decisions made during the evolution of KnowledgeOS.

Each ADR explains:

- Context
- Decision
- Consequences
- Alternatives

ADR are immutable.

If a decision changes, a new ADR must supersede the previous one.
