# Scope

## Status

Draft — pending content.

## Purpose

This document defines the scope of the KnowledgeOS V-3 Master Library module: what is included in V-3, what is explicitly out of scope, and the constraints that shape those decisions. It is the boundary contract between the Master Library and the rest of the system.

## Sections

- **In-scope capabilities** — the features and behaviors that V-3 must deliver.
- **Out-of-scope capabilities** — features intentionally deferred or excluded, with rationale.
- **Constraints** — technical, organizational, or timeline constraints that shape scope decisions.
- **Interfaces to other modules** — the explicit contracts or surfaces that bound this module.
