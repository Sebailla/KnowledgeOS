# ImplementationStrategy

## Status

Draft — pending content.

## Purpose

This document defines the overall implementation strategy for the KnowledgeOS V-3 project, including sequencing, scope boundaries, and the guiding principles that shape how work is planned and executed. It is the entry point for understanding how the modules documented in `00-Governance` and `01-MasterLibrary` will be built.

## Sections

- **Guiding principles** — the engineering principles that constrain design and implementation choices.
- **Workstreams** — the parallel tracks that divide the project into independently executable units.
- **Sequencing** — the order in which workstreams are scheduled and the dependencies between them.
- **Risk and assumptions** — known risks, mitigations, and assumptions that the strategy depends on.
