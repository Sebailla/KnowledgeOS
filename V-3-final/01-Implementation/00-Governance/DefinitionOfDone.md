## Purpose

This document captures the Definition of Done used across the KnowledgeOS V-3 project: the checklist every work item must satisfy before it is considered complete. It is the governance counterpart to the per-module lifecycle and serves as a shared acceptance gate.

## Sections

- **Global Definition of Done** — non-negotiable criteria that apply to every change.
- **Documentation requirements** — what must accompany a change to user-facing or technical docs.
- **Quality gates** — automated checks (tests, lint, types) that must pass.
- **Review and approval** — the human sign-off steps required to close a work item.
