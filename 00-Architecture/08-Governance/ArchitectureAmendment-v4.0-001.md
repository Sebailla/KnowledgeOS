# Architecture Amendment v4.0-001

**Project:** KnowledgeOS  
**Section:** Governance  
**Document:** ArchitectureAmendment-v4.0-001  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Status:** Release Candidate  
**Author:** KnowledgeOS Team  

---

## 1. Purpose

Formally incorporate the Master Library, selective Local Libraries and Personal Knowledge synchronization model into the V4 governance baseline.

## 2. Amendment

Architecture V4 SHALL interpret library authority as scoped:

- Master Library owns Master Catalog and source publications.
- Local Libraries own local membership and availability.
- Personal Knowledge remains user-owned.
- Personal Knowledge synchronizes only among approved Local Libraries.
- Publication acquisition is explicit and separate from synchronization.

## 3. Superseded Interpretations

Any prior wording describing Local Libraries as Master replicas, NAS as a Personal synchronization peer or one global Source of Truth is superseded.

## 4. Affected Areas

Foundation, Domain, Platform Library, Platform Sync, Integration Synchronization, C4, UML and ADR-004/008/009 interpretations.

## 5. Status

Accepted as part of the V4 baseline.
