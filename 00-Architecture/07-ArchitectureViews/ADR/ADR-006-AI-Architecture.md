# ADR-006 — AI Architecture

**Project:** KnowledgeOS  
**Section:** Architecture Views / ADR  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Status:** Accepted  
**Author:** KnowledgeOS Team  

---

## 1. Context

KnowledgeOS must support local and remote AI while protecting privacy and authority.

## 2. Decision

Use provider-neutral AI Engine with policy-controlled local/remote execution. AI output is derived or Personal Knowledge until explicitly accepted.

## 3. Consequences

Provides flexibility and privacy; requires provenance, model versioning and validation.

## 4. Supersession

None

## 5. Status

**Accepted**
